# AX·DX 교육 히어로 아트디렉션 — Round 2

## A. Editorial Evidence

1. **핵심**: 교육의 분위기보다 실무 결과, 사례, 축적된 증거를 먼저 보여주는 편집 아카이브.
2. **ImageGen 프롬프트**: 아래 전문.
3. **팔레트**: Bone `#E5E0D6`, Carbon `#232321`, Sage `#8A9684`, Vermilion `#B6412E`.
4. **한글 서체 성격**: 좁고 단단한 제목용 고딕 + 중립적인 본문 고딕. 보고서 인상을 내되 관공서 서식처럼 보이지 않는 활자.
5. **스크롤 모션**: 증거 레이어가 깊이 순서대로 열리고 붉은 원이 핵심 결과를 짚는 방식.
6. **사람·수제 느낌**: 실제 손의 기록 장면, 스테이플과 종이 구멍, 불균일한 인쇄와 아카이브 재료.
7. **장점**: ‘교육을 들었다’보다 ‘업무 결과가 남는다’는 B2B 설득 논리가 강함.
8. **위험**: 이미지가 지나치게 문화·건축 잡지처럼 느껴지면 교육 상품의 즉시성이 약해질 수 있음.
9. **스마트메이커 난점**: 다층 마스크, 서로 다른 깊이의 시차, 비정형 아카이브 레이어의 반응형 재배치.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: responsive AX·DX corporate education landing page hero, Round 2 concept A “Editorial Evidence”
Primary request: Create a bold evidence-driven editorial hero showing that practical education produces visible organizational results. Do not depict a classroom, workshop table, generic office scene, workflow machine, or conventional corporate training photography.
Scene/backdrop: an oversized vertical archive installation made from blank case-study sheets, embossed tabs without characters, translucent proof layers, specimen pockets, punched paper edges, a single red verification seal made only as an abstract circle, and cropped monochrome documentary fragments of real hands using tools. The archive feels curated like an independent architecture magazine exhibition.
Subject: tangible evidence, before-and-after material contrast, accumulated output, annotated-looking composition using only abstract lines and shapes with zero readable characters.
Style/medium: high-end editorial art direction, architectural exhibition photography mixed with restrained risograph texture and documentary fragments; intelligent, tactile, slightly raw, never glossy AI imagery.
Composition/framing: 16:9 wide landscape, target 2048×1152. Reserve a calm pale-gray copy field on the left 40%. Build one tall asymmetrical evidence tower on center-right, keeping its strongest proof layers inside the central 55% for a portrait mobile crop. Strong scale contrast, not repeated cards.
Lighting/mood: raking museum light, credible, investigative, precise, quietly provocative.
Color palette: bone gray, carbon black, muted sage, one vermilion-red accent.
Materials/textures: heavyweight report paper, blind embossing, tracing film, staple metal, archival cloth, coarse risograph ink.
Text: none.
Constraints: absolutely no Korean or English text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, readable charts, fake UI, dates, prices, headings, stamps with writing, or QR codes. No rocket, space, planet, light streak, robot, AI orb, circuit brain, purple cyberpunk, neon, glassmorphism, dashboard, bento grid, classroom, laptop-centered desk, posed people, or stock photography. Mobile-safe composition and generous copy space.
```

## B. Spatial Curriculum

1. **핵심**: 교육 과정을 설명표가 아니라 서로 다른 재료의 문을 통과하는 공간적 성장 경로로 표현.
2. **ImageGen 프롬프트**: 아래 전문.
3. **팔레트**: Limestone `#D7C8AE`, Timber `#A9875E`, Teal `#7F9994`, Cobalt `#173B66`.
4. **한글 서체 성격**: 건축 편집물처럼 넓은 여백을 살리는 기하학적 고딕과 짧은 행장.
5. **스크롤 모션**: 스크롤할수록 카메라가 다음 문으로 이동하고 재료가 거친 것에서 정밀한 것으로 변화.
6. **사람·수제 느낌**: 작은 한 사람의 실제 이동, 목재와 종이 펄프의 표면, 긴 자연광 그림자.
7. **장점**: 커리큘럼, 성장, 미래 준비를 문장 없이도 한 장면에 담으며 흔한 교육 사진을 피함.
8. **위험**: 실제 교육 공간 또는 부동산 이미지로 오해될 수 있어 HTML 카피와의 결속이 중요함.
9. **스마트메이커 난점**: 카메라 전진형 스크롤, 여러 문을 관통하는 원근 유지, 재료별 깊이 효과.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: responsive AX·DX corporate education landing page hero, Round 2 concept B “Spatial Curriculum”
Primary request: Visualize learning as a physical route through a sequence of thresholds, decisions, and capability levels. Create an architectural hero, not a classroom, office, workflow diagram, or website UI.
Scene/backdrop: a daylight gallery-scale installation of five connected spatial frames and platforms, each made from a different practical material—paper pulp, timber, frosted glass, brushed aluminum, and blue textile. The path advances from low rough forms to precise elevated forms, suggesting AX·DX capability growth without literal icons or signs.
Subject: one small, naturally proportioned adult figure moving through the middle threshold for scale, occupying less than 8% of the visual. No close face, no visible hands.
Style/medium: architectural model photography blended with real full-scale installation; refined contemporary Korean museum scenography; tactile and believable, not futuristic.
Composition/framing: 16:9 wide landscape, target 2048×1152. Use deep perspective from lower-right toward upper-center while leaving the entire left 38% as a calm warm-stone wall for HTML copy. Keep the figure and central threshold inside the central 45% for a portrait mobile crop.
Lighting/mood: long natural morning shadows, calm progression, clarity, confidence, future readiness without spectacle.
Color palette: warm limestone, pale sand, oxidized teal, deep cobalt, charcoal.
Materials/textures: mineral plaster, raw timber grain, paper pulp, woven textile, satin aluminum, frosted glass.
Text: none.
Constraints: no Korean or English text, letters, numbers, signage, logos, watermark, readable UI, fake dashboard, arrows, schedules, or prices. No rocket, space, planet, speed streak, robot, glowing sphere, circuit brain, purple cyberpunk, neon, glassmorphism, bento grid, classroom, desks, laptops, charts, posed team, or stock-photo look. Ensure the route reads spatially and remains usable in a vertical mobile crop.
```

## C. Collective Intelligence

1. **핵심**: 다양한 사람이 서로 다른 물성을 연결해 하나의 작동 구조를 완성하는 집단지성의 탑뷰.
2. **ImageGen 프롬프트**: 아래 전문.
3. **팔레트**: Cobalt `#004DC1`, Chartreuse `#CEEF32`, Paper `#E4D7BD`, Charcoal `#30323B`.
4. **한글 서체 성격**: 크고 직접적인 제목용 고딕, 짧고 선명한 문장, 스포츠·문화 포스터에 가까운 활력.
5. **스크롤 모션**: 네 손과 재료가 시간차로 진입하고 형광 끈이 마지막에 순환 구조를 완성.
6. **사람·수제 느낌**: 서로 다른 피부와 손, 재료를 실제로 잡는 힘, 종이·목재·금속의 물리적 연결.
7. **장점**: 사람과 AI의 협업을 로봇이나 화면 없이 가장 직접적이고 기억하기 쉽게 표현.
8. **위험**: 밝은 색과 탑뷰가 책임자 교육보다 젊은 캠페인처럼 보일 수 있음.
9. **스마트메이커 난점**: 네 방향에서 진입하는 오브젝트 애니메이션, 끈 경로 변화, 모바일 재구성.

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: responsive AX·DX corporate education landing page hero, Round 2 concept C “Collective Intelligence”
Primary request: Create an overhead editorial composition about people and AI improving work together through coordinated action. It must feel like a bold cultural magazine cover image, not a workshop photograph, classroom, office, dashboard, or workflow machine.
Scene/backdrop: a single matte cobalt floor plane viewed perfectly from above. Four adult participants are represented only by natural forearms and anatomically correct hands entering from different edges, jointly arranging a small set of blank physical objects: a translucent disk, a folded aluminum sheet, a rough paper module, a wooden connector, and a single chartreuse cord. The objects form one purposeful shared system.
Subject: coordinated human action and complementary materials; no faces, no bodies, no computers, no documents with marks. Hands occupy under 22% of the frame and must have correct fingers, joints, scale, and grip.
Style/medium: crisp overhead editorial photography with subtle screen-print color blocking and real tactile shadows; contemporary, human, direct, slightly playful but credible for senior corporate education.
Composition/framing: 16:9 wide landscape, target 2048×1152. Reserve the left 38% as mostly uninterrupted cobalt negative space for HTML copy. Put the collaborative object cluster in center-right while keeping the core disk, cord, and two key hands within the central 50% for a portrait crop. Asymmetric, iconic, not a grid.
Lighting/mood: hard-but-natural side light, clear shadows, decisive, collaborative, optimistic.
Color palette: saturated cobalt, warm skin tones, chartreuse cord, raw aluminum, unbleached paper, dark charcoal sleeves.
Materials/textures: matte painted floor, skin texture, paper fiber, brushed metal, wood grain, translucent resin, woven cord.
Text: none.
Constraints: absolutely no Korean or English text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, readable UI, fake dashboard, charts, dates, prices, sticky notes, notebooks, laptops, screens, or labels. No rocket, space, planet, light streak, robot, AI sphere, circuit brain, purple cyberpunk, neon, glassmorphism, bento grid, posed team portrait, or glossy stock look. Every hand must be anatomically plausible. Preserve mobile-safe central action and generous copy space.
```

## D. Quiet Instruments

1. **핵심**: 사람이나 화면 없이 판단·측정·연결·변환을 네 개의 도구와 실제 빛으로 표현.
2. **ImageGen 프롬프트**: 아래 전문.
3. **팔레트**: Carbon `#11120F`, Ivory `#EEE7D8`, Silver `#AEB3B5`, Amber `#B86D19`.
4. **한글 서체 성격**: 저채도 화면과 대비되는 정밀한 산세리프, 작은 정보는 넓은 자간으로 절제.
5. **스크롤 모션**: 좁은 빛이 이동하면서 유리 굴절이 변하고 연결 끈에 미세한 장력이 생김.
6. **사람·수제 느낌**: 종이 섬유, 수제 유리의 미세 기포, 금속 가공 흔적, 먼지와 필름 그레인.
7. **장점**: 흔한 AI 이미지에서 가장 멀고, 책임자·프리미엄 교육에 어울리는 차분한 지적 인상을 줌.
8. **위험**: 교육이나 협업을 즉시 이해하기 어렵고, 제품 광고 또는 라이프스타일 브랜드로 오해될 수 있음.
9. **스마트메이커 난점**: 실제 굴절광 변화, 미세 광원 이동, 저조도에서 텍스트 대비를 안정적으로 유지하는 반응형 처리.

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: responsive AX·DX corporate education landing page hero, Round 2 concept D “Quiet Instruments”
Primary request: Create a radically minimal, people-free hero that expresses human judgment working with advanced tools through carefully arranged physical instruments. It should feel like a premium industrial-design journal, not AI art, a classroom, office, dashboard, workflow, archive wall, or architecture scene.
Scene/backdrop: a charcoal-black mineral surface holding only four precisely composed objects: a thin folded sheet of warm paper, a small brushed-steel calibration block, a translucent amber glass lens casting a real caustic shadow, and a dark-blue woven cord connecting two objects. One narrow line of daylight moves across the materials.
Subject: quiet relationship between knowledge, measurement, connection, and transformed light; no screens and no people.
Style/medium: ultra-refined still-life photography with analog large-format film grain, Japanese and Swiss industrial editorial restraint, real optical behavior, tactile imperfection, minimal but not sterile.
Composition/framing: 16:9 wide landscape, target 2048×1152. Keep the left 40% nearly empty and dark for HTML copy. Place the four-object constellation low center-right, with the lens and connecting cord inside the central 50% for a portrait crop. Strong negative space and one surprising amber light shape.
Lighting/mood: controlled natural slit light, contemplative, intelligent, exact, confident, premium without luxury clichés.
Color palette: carbon black, warm paper ivory, brushed silver, deep indigo, amber glass.
Materials/textures: mineral stone, fibrous paper edge, machined steel, woven cord, hand-cast glass, subtle dust and film grain.
Text: none.
Constraints: absolutely no Korean or English text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, UI, screen, dashboard, chart, date, price, label, engraved markings, or fake product branding. No rocket, space, planet, light streak, robot, AI sphere, circuit brain, purple cyberpunk, neon, glassmorphism, bento grid, classroom, office, laptop, hand, face, or stock-photo person. Avoid generic luxury perfume imagery. Maintain real-world material plausibility, generous copy space, and mobile crop safety.
```
